## The trial

Every new install gets **14 days of Pro**, no credit card. When it ends, Junction drops to Free — nothing is deleted, and buying a plan unlocks everything again instantly.

## Activating a license

After purchase, your license key arrives by email (from `no-reply@junction.tuntau.com`) and appears in your account dashboard.

1. In Junction, open **Settings → License** (or the plans dialog).
2. Paste the key, which starts with `JUNCTION-`.
3. Click **Activate**.

Keys are verified **offline** with a cryptographic signature, so activation works without internet.

Every 2–4 weeks Junction checks the key with `junction.tuntau.com` (it sends the key and a random install ID — nothing else). That's how revoked or over-shared keys stop working. **Offline for a while? No problem:** your plan keeps working for **60 days** after the last successful check, and Settings warns you two weeks before that runs out.

## Devices and seats

One seat is one person. You can use your key on up to **5 devices** — work laptop, home desktop, a Linux box. Moving to a new machine? Just activate there.

## Managing billing

**Settings → License → Manage billing** opens your account dashboard: change plan, update payment details, download invoices or cancel. Cancelling a subscription keeps Pro until the end of the period you paid for.
